05 / THREAT INTELLIGENCE

Threat Intelligence Desk.

Enrich observables, understand adversary behavior and connect external intelligence with internal telemetry.

ANALYST WORKFLOW

Observable → context → action.

Collect → Validate → Enrich → Correlate → Analyze → Act

Use external intelligence as investigation context, then validate it against the user, host, process, time window and surrounding telemetry.

ANALYST PRINCIPLE

An IOC is a lead, not a verdict.

An IP, domain, URL or hash match should be investigated in context. Reputation and intelligence sources can support a hypothesis, but internal evidence determines the incident decision.
