05 / THREAT INTELLIGENCE

Threat Intelligence Desk.

Turn indicators, adversary activity and threat reporting into investigation context.

INTELLIGENCE LIFECYCLE

From observable to action.

Collect → Validate → Enrich → Correlate → Analyze → Disseminate → Act

Use external intelligence to add context, then validate it against internal telemetry before making an incident decision.

ANALYST PRINCIPLE

An IOC is a lead.

An IP, domain or hash match is not automatically a confirmed incident. Validate time, user, host, process, source context and follow-on activity.

01 / THREAT ACTORS

Know the adversary.

APT Groups

Track aliases, targets, motivations, techniques and known infrastructure.

Ransomware Groups

Understand access, execution, lateral movement, extortion and impact patterns.

Initial Access Brokers

Follow access methods, exposed services, credentials and downstream activity.

Cybercrime & Hacktivism

Track campaigns, motivations, infrastructure and operational patterns.

02 / CAMPAIGNS

Campaign intelligence.

Campaign Overview

Target sectors, geography, timing, motivation and known activity.

Attack Chain

Initial access → execution → persistence → credential access → C2 → impact.

Detection Opportunities

Map observed behavior to telemetry, ATT&CK techniques and detection ideas.

03 / IOC INTELLIGENCE

Observable enrichment.

IP

Reputation, ASN, geolocation, scanning context and related infrastructure.

Domain / URL

DNS history, registration context, redirects, hosting and related indicators.

Hash

Malware family, detections, sandbox behavior and related samples.

Email & Certificate

Sender infrastructure, certificate relationships and campaign pivots.

Connect intelligence to analytics, hunting hypotheses and gaps in visibility.
