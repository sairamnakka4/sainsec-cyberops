06 / CTI

Threat Intelligence Desk.

Use threat intelligence to add context to observables and guide investigation—not as a substitute for internal validation.

IOC workflow

Observable → enrich → reputation/context → related infrastructure → correlate with internal telemetry.

An IP, domain or hash becomes useful when combined with time, user, host, process and surrounding activity.

Analyst rule

An IOC hit is a lead, not automatically a confirmed incident.

Validate the user, asset, process, time window, source context and follow-on activity before escalating.

COMMON PIVOTS

What to enrich.

IP

Reputation, ASN, scanning context, related infrastructure and observed services.

Domain

DNS history, registration context, redirects, hosting and related indicators.

Hash

Malware detections, sandbox behavior, family/context and related files.

URL

Redirect chain, page behavior, domains, IPs and phishing indicators.
